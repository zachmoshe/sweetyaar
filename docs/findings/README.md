# Real-life findings

Results of experiments run on a bench device that are worth knowing for anyone
working on SweetYaar. Each finding is a short report written as a record of what was
measured, with its setup, results and recommendations.

Each finding has its own folder, with the final PDF report and raw data when relevant.

| Date | Finding | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11 | [Battery runtime under continuous playback](battery-under-stress/battery-under-stress.pdf) | A full charge lasts about 34 h of SD card playback and 15 h of Bluetooth streaming; warn at 3.48 V (90 min left) and 3.39 V (30 min left). |
